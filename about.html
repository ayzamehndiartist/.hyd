About Ayza

Ayza is a henna artist based in Hyderabad, Telangana. She has over four years of experience and specializes in bridal, party and event mehndi.

Her work covers Bridal, Arabic, Traditional, Engagement, Party and Event mehndi, so there is a style for most occasions. She also offers home service.

To book an appointment, fill out the booking form when it is available. You can see more of her work on Instagram and in the gallery.
